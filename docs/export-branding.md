# Your match. Your video. Your branding.

Roundnet's official local editor is free to use, with unlimited exports. We do
not add our logo, a watermark, an advertisement, a promotional intro or outro,
or a required attribution screen to exported recordings.

Scoreboards, statistics cards, captions, team graphics, and sponsor logos are
the editor's choice. Users can export a clean cut or include the presentation
elements they select. No payment is required to remove Roundnet branding.

Editing and rendering run locally. No account or cloud upload is required for
the local editing workflow. Roundnet does not claim ownership of the footage
you import or the videos you export.

The project may be supported through voluntary contributions, sponsorships,
paid support, or optional services. Those offers belong outside exported
videos. If we introduce hosted services, local editing and clean exports will
remain available without purchasing them.
